How acupuncture helps

Complementing your medical care

For couples who have been trying to conceive for some time, or who are undergoing assisted fertility or IVF, acupuncture complements standard medical care and can be used safely alongside medications and hormone treatments.

The clinical and experimental evidence suggests acupuncture supports fertility by:

  • Modulating the balance of hormones, particularly helpful with PCOS and endometriosis
  • Increasing blood flow to the reproductive organs
  • Down-regulating inflammation
  • Improving sperm maturation and supporting sperm production and quality
  • Helping manage the negative effects of stress

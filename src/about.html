Beginnings

It started in Japan, in 1999

I was working in Japan as a language teacher, often overworked, stressed and tired. A colleague suggested acupuncture to restore my energy and relieve my stress. I was sceptical, and concerned about the needles.

My first practitioner, Manabu, was blind, and my scepticism doubled. Yet after just one session I could face my daily challenges with renewed energy. A few more, and I was back to my optimistic, energetic self.

The journey

How I got here

1999 · Japan

A first experience of acupuncture

One session with a blind practitioner changed how I felt, and what I wanted to do with my life.

Brisbane, Australia

Four years studying Traditional Chinese Medicine

Full-time at the Australian College of Natural Medicine (now Endeavour College of Natural Health), covering Eastern and Western medicine and hundreds of hours of clinical practice. Graduated with the "Most Outstanding Student Practitioner of Acupuncture" award.

Brisbane & the Caribbean

Clinics, then "Resident Acupuncturist at Sea"

After practising in Brisbane clinics, I worked on a cruise ship, where relaxed patients surrounded by water were remarkably receptive to treatment.

2011 · York

York Traditional Acupuncture opens

Back in the city where I studied History of Art (1995–98), I set up my own practice and have since treated hundreds of people.

Working as a team

Part of your wider care

I receive referrals from doctors, pharmacists, osteopaths, stop-smoking services and nutritionists. If you'd also benefit from another therapy, I'll happily refer you to the professionals I work closely with. And if it's best for you to see your GP for further investigation, I'll recommend that too.

Special interests include breaking nicotine addiction, weight loss, women's health and pregnancy, fatigue, back pain, migraines and respiratory conditions.
